What is Lowell

Lowell high school is a merit based public high school that is reportedly the best in San Francisco and one of the best in California. Students are required to write a series of small paragraphs and where grades are considered when applying.

The school offers many clubs. They are known to have over one hundred clubs. The school offers many classes as well, especially honors and advanced placement classes. It's known that the classes are academically challenging.

Student Government

At Lowell, each grade level gets their own student government board to look over their respective grade level with an exception of the student body council in which they look over the entire student body. Each board contains six students officers, president, vice president, treasurer, secretary, historian, and public relations. Our boards are called 'LSA boards' which stands for Lowell Student Association.

The Student Body Council and our Lowell Student Associations together plan school events such as rallies, dances, certain fundraisers, and much more. Additionally, they represent the student body for people outside of Lowell. They communicate with alumni, internships, and many more people to help support the student body.
