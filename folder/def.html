What is Key Club?

Key Club is one of the oldest volunteering organizations in the world that originated in Sacromento, California in 1925. It is a student-led organization that provides its members with opportunities to perform service, build character, and develop leadership skills. Key Club is a part of Kiwanis International, which is a global organization of volunteers.
