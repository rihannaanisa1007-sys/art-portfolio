About me

Hello my name is Aneesah. I'm a high school student at Lowell High school and this is my Graphic Design portfolio to demonstrate my skills and how I've grown over time.

I've been doing graphic design since I was thirteen and I've been doing it pretty often ever since. I've had multiple positions as a graphic designer at my current high school and I hope to do it more seriously in the future.

For my entire life, I've been doing art. Any type of art, 2D or 3D, I love doing it all and I'm open to trying new art forms. I've had many pieces displayed in the SFmoma, around twice times now. I had three of poems published, two of them in books. Additionally, I've had multiple videos published as well with some gaining a million views.

What is the Purpose of this Website?

This website serves as a portfolio to showcase my graphic design skills and projects. Projects include posters, graphics, posts, and more for school clubs and organizations. Additionally, this website allows me to see my growth and development overtime.

The History Behind it All

As stated earlier, I started this around 2023 when I was in my last year of middle school. I found the world of graphic design after I was assigned the task of creating posters for my paid internship about nutrition. The goal was to promote an upcoming event for artists to draw something for the chance to get it displayed on muni buses.

Entering Freshman year, I took a break on graphic design and did video editing for Key club. I was an intern for one of Senior officers of that club and made promotional videos. Later that year I took over their role completely and continuing it during Sophomore year.

During Sophomore year, I was the Publicity Committee Chair for the same club but on a higher level. Meaning that I went above the school level, called the 'Division.' It supervised all of Key clubs in San Francisco and South City. Being the Committee Chair, I also looked over my committee's graphics that had three people, to make sure they were appropriate and looked visually appealing.

Coming into Junior year, I am continuing my role as the Publicity Committee chair with an additional chair. Additionally I am the Public Relations for Lowell's student government for the class of 2028.
